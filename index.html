Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury fragrances

Are your perfumes long-lasting?

Yes, all True Fragrance perfumes are formulated with high-grade oils (Parfum concentration), ensuring they last 12-24 hours on skin and even longer on fabric.

Are your ingredients vegan and cruelty-free?

Absolutely. We are committed to ethical sourcing. Our fragrances are 100% vegan, cruelty-free, and crafted using high-quality plant-based ethanol.

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery across Pakistan takes 3-5 business days. We offer free delivery on all orders above Rs. 5,000.

Can I return or exchange a perfume?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery, provided the perfume is unused, sealed, and in its original premium packaging.
